What to look for

Parallax. Drag sideways. The barrel at 0.6 m sweeps across; the oil scene behind barely shifts. Pan enters the projection before the divide by depth, so the separation is real rather than a slide.

Convergence. The grid lines are not drawn converging — they are drawn straight in metres and converge because the projection puts them there.

The flat scene is one sheet. Push scene depth back and the whole oil painting recedes together. That is the honest limit of billboarding hand-drawn art, and why the derrick and barrel are separate props.

v4 depth sign flips the projection to the prototype's phone-view formula, d = 6.5 − z. Watch the far scene lunge to the front while the near barrel retreats — that is the contradiction we found between v4's two views, and the reason this build standardises on +z = farther.
